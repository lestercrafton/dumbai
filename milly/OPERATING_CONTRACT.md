# Milly: independent weekly forecasts

Milly's published twenty come from her own chronologically evaluated historical model. Salary constrains lineups; it is not a player forecast. Analyst point projections and customer beliefs are not baseline features. All three FLEX constructions receive comparable search effort, with no forced quota in the selected twenty.

Customer-described worlds are separate conditional experiments. Interpretations are shown before generation, unsupported statements are rejected, and simulated reference-field results are not real Millionaire Maker probabilities. Optional local belief records precede the displayed result.

A release requires fresh official slate identity, roster legality checks, separate selection and evaluation simulations, a provenance receipt, and a dated immutable archive. Failed runs must keep previous results explicitly dated, not present them as refreshed.

Weekly execution is only considered active after a real external workflow is installed and an execution receipt verifies its success. Schedule configuration alone is not a completed model run.
