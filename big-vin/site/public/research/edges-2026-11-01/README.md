# Edge research: openers and soft markets (Nov 1, 2026)

Research only. Nothing live changed. Models: NBA v2 and CBB v2, walk-forward daily refits (only games before each date).
Lines: ESPN BET opening and closing lines from ESPN's free odds endpoint. Grading is at −110 unless a column says the listed opener price.
95% intervals come from 2,000 bootstrap resamples. Rules were picked on 2024-25 only; 2025-26 is the holdout.

## Verdicts
1. **Betting the opener across the board: no.** The NBA ≥4 tier was 54.1% in selection and 53.9% in holdout, with every interval crossing 52.4%. CBB never cleared 52.4% at any threshold in the selection season.
2. **CBB neutral-site games at edge ≥3: maybe, the one candidate worth live paper-tracking.** Selection: 147–115, 56.1% [50.0–62.2]. Holdout: 110–80, 57.9% [51.1–64.7], +20.0u, ROI +10.5% [−2.5, +23.6]. Average CLV was +2.6 points, so the market moves to our side. The ROI interval still includes losing, and about 30 splits were tested, so one survivor could be chance.
3. **NBA totals at edge ≥8: maybe, weak.** Selection 59.1% [49.5–68.8]; holdout 56.9% [48.0–65.0], +10.6u on 123 bets. Small sample.
4. **Rules that passed selection and then failed holdout:** NBA favorites at edge ≥2 (57.4% → 46.6%) and CBB low-major vs low-major (53.5% → 49.6%).
5. **Opener bettability:** ESPN's odds data has no timestamps and no limits, and its line-movement endpoint returned nothing. ESPN BET's opener is not necessarily the market's first opener. Opener limits are usually small. Treat all opener results as an upper bound.
6. **First-half and team totals: no data.** ESPN's free odds give full-game spread and total only (prop bets are player props). Quarter scores are free in ESPN summaries, so a half model could be built, but grading it needs a free archive of historical first-half and team-total lines. I did not find one.
