# Re-score of the October 16 tuning (for Lester's decision)

Generated 2026-10-19. Paper results, entertainment only.

## What was tuned
On 2026-10-16 the NFL, CFB and WNBA models switched to `*-season-tuned-2026-10-16`. The settings were picked by a grid search (half-life × rating penalty) whose objective was net units on the **same 2026 games** it was then reported on (`big-vin/site/scripts/backtest-2026-10-16.mjs`). Recorded in-sample gains: NFL −2.79u → +10.67u (64 games); CFB +21.95u → +33.77u (367 games).

## Out-of-sample evidence
From the five-season NFL strict walk-forward (`/workspace/walkforward/RESULTS.md`, pre-registered in `PREREGISTRATION.md`):
- The Oct 16 settings show 53-33-6, +15.1u on 2026 weeks 1-6 there, but that is the same data they were tuned on. It is in-sample and not evidence.
- Across 423 challenger proposals from the same parameter space, ideas averaged +2.5% ROI on the two trailing seasons that picked them, then −5.3% over the next 17 weeks. The frozen baseline did −5.1% over the same windows, so nothing carried over.
- The best of 1,440 configs on 2026 shows +26.6% ROI on 50 picks with hindsight. Only 36% of configs are positive in 2026.
- 2021-2025: every model lost about the vig (−3.6% to −6.1% ROI). The baseline's 2026 run (60.5%, p ≈ 0.08) looks like variance.
- CLV can't be computed for these games: only first-seen prices were saved, and no closing lines. The append-only odds log starting 2026-10-19 fixes this from now on.

Forward games since activation (site `/api/results`, live cohort, as of 2026-10-19 00:30 CT): CFB 61-46-2, +9.52u on 109 priced picks (one weekend, no CLV, no paired baseline). NFL: none graded yet under the tuned version. WNBA: 0-1.

## Verdict
The Oct 16 tuning **does not hold up as evidence of an improvement**. Its gain is in-sample, and the walk-forward shows that this kind of parameter selection doesn't persist. One forward CFB weekend is far below any sample that would mean anything.

**Not reverted.** Reverting is also a model change, and the experiment rules say promotions and reversions are dated new versions decided by the owner. Options for Lester:
1. Keep the tuned settings as Playbook v1 (current state, frozen 2026-10-19), and judge them only on forward games against the `ridge-v1` baseline mirror.
2. Register a dated new version that returns NFL/CFB/WNBA to the Sep 14 / ridge-v1 settings, effective at a stated future time.
