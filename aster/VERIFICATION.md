# Release verification — The Last Bell of Xenaland

Verified September 17, 2026 (America/Chicago), after the early-reader family revision.

- **Engine:** 12 tests passed. Complete Easy play and More challenge routes, every optional gift, skipping gifts, alternative upgrades, both wizard phases, resource limits, stop/defend/heal effects, retry checkpoints, immutable state, save validation, and old-save migration.
- **Previous saves:** 263 actual checkpoints generated by the first-release engine migrated successfully. Encounter, turn, earned notes, choices, upgrades, health, magic, shields, enemy health and already-announced damage were preserved. Opening and finale story pages restart with the new family context; other active story pages use their updated copy.
- **Desktop browser:** Chromium at 1280×800. A fresh game completed through visible DOM controls: 117 actions, 35 battle turns, six battles, six gifts, two upgrades and both Vadish phases. All 62 displayed story screens were recorded, including the nine finale screens.
- **Saving:** Reload during battle 1, turn 3 restored the exact revised save. The ending was revisited from the title and after reload.
- **Controls:** Tutorial, help, journal, settings, sound, reduced motion, battle notes, credits and return buttons worked.
- **Mobile:** Chromium at 390×844. All five battle moves were readable, unobstructed and reachable. No horizontal overflow; the taller ending scrolls normally. A Shield tap advanced the turn.
- **Visuals:** New child portraits of Oshn and Isha inspected; real alpha transparency verified. Desktop title, battle, Vadish’s second phase, finale and mobile battle/ending inspected for clipping and overlap. The finale shows Oshn, Isha as a girl, and Vadish together.
- **Errors:** No console errors, page errors or failed network requests in the final full desktop and mobile runs.
- **Story:** Oshn is a seven-year-old boy with warm brown skin. Isha is his sister, under a temporary moth spell. The story uses short sentences, a clear six-note quest and a happy family ending. After his defeat, Vadish becomes loving and rings the great bell himself beside Oshn and Isha. Their mother welcomes them home for pancakes.

Checks use both the deterministic engine and actual browser clicks. They do not jump directly to the ending to claim a complete playthrough. The game is static and has no external gameplay service. Progress belongs to the browser that saved it. Physical touchscreen hardware and Mobile Safari were not separately tested.
